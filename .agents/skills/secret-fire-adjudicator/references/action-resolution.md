# Action Resolution

## Modes

- `automatic`: No meaningful resistance or uncertainty remains. Use `success`; omit a primary attribute.
- `contested`: A person, hazard, time pressure, limited resource, or uncertain execution can materially oppose the method. Select exactly one primary attribute.
- `impossible`: The stated method cannot reach the desired result under established facts. Explain the concrete boundary; do not reinterpret failure as stupidity or punish experimentation.
- Clarification belongs to `action_frame.mode=clarify`, not to the resolution object. Use it only when materially different readings could cause an important irreversible consequence and no minimal reversible reading is possible.

## Outcomes

- `impossible`: The method cannot begin or cannot produce the intended kind of result.
- `blocked`: The attempt meets its first boundary without achieving the goal.
- `limited_success`: The immediate goal is partly achieved, or achieved with an established cost, reduced scope, or new exposure.
- `success`: The immediate goal is achieved within the stated boundary.
- `strong_success`: The method and circumstances justify a clearly better immediate result, never an unrelated bonus.

## Contextual Judgment

For a close contest, weigh recorded training, equipment, injuries, loads, preparation, position, environment, knowledge, motives, loyalties, and current attention. These facts may narrow or broaden outcomes but must not silently create capabilities.

The 20-point rule applies only when both sides directly oppose one another with the compared capability and no method-changing fact alters the problem. If the lower side attacks the higher side's strength head-on, success is unavailable. If the player instead changes the objective or method, reassess the new contest rather than carrying the old gap across automatically.

Examples:

- Convincing a loyal guard to betray an oath immediately is usually impossible even with high charisma unless leverage, motive, or relationship makes betrayal plausible.
- A tired archmage can fail to stop an escape when divided attention, reluctance, terrain, or a prepared route changes the problem; this is not necessarily a direct mystic-power contest.
- High intellect without recorded medicine does not permit complex surgery.
- A locked door may be opened automatically with its key, contested with lockpicks and training, or impossible to punch through for an ordinary person.
